Frequently Asked Questions

How does ReviewIntel analyze reviews?

ReviewIntel uses advanced AI (Claude 3.5 Sonnet) to analyze Amazon review content and metadata. It identifies patterns, themes, and sentiment across 50+ reviews to extract actionable insights. The entire analysis is completed in 60-90 seconds.

What data do I need to provide?

Simply provide your Amazon product ASIN (the 10-character product ID) or a link to your product page. ReviewIntel will automatically fetch the latest reviews and analyze them.

Can I analyze competitor products?

Yes! You can analyze any Amazon product by its ASIN. This is great for competitive research and understanding market gaps.

How are reports delivered?

Reports are delivered as professional PDF documents via email within 2 minutes of completing your analysis. You also get access to view and download reports from your dashboard.

What's included in a report?

Each report includes: Top 5 positive themes, Top 5 negative themes, 5 specific recommendations with impact/effort ratings, sentiment breakdown, competitor mentions, and trend analysis.

Is there a free trial?

Yes! Enter your email above to get a free sample report with mock data. This shows you exactly what a full report looks like. No credit card required.

Can I get a refund?

If you're not satisfied with your report within 7 days, we offer a full refund. For subscriptions, cancel anytime - you'll have access through the end of your billing cycle.

How often should I run analyses?

We recommend analyzing your products monthly to track sentiment trends and monitor the impact of improvements. Quarterly analysis is minimum for tracking meaningful changes.
